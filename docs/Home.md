# Minecraft ARK Wiki

Minecraft ARK is a prepared survival adventure world for Java 26.3 and Bedrock
through Geyser/Floodgate. You need your own server. Documentation is in English;
in-game content and the administration panel are currently primarily German.

1. [Download the world](World-Download.md) and verify its checksums.
2. [Install](Installation.md), generate your own secrets and start the server.
3. [Play](Playing.md): atlas, quests, land claims, dinosaurs and the creative portal.
4. [Administer](Administration.md), back up and change your password.
5. [Troubleshoot](Troubleshooting.md) or [build the source](Development.md).
