# Jurassic archipelago: source register

Research checked on 2026-11-22. This is a reconstruction reference register,
not evidence that a location has been built or accepted. Actual coordinates,
compressed distances and connections are this project's design decisions.

## Evidence order and limits

1. The released films control visible scene appearance and film attribution.
2. Production material and studio sources can explain visible work, but a
   synopsis does not establish a building plan or island-wide coordinates.
3. Licensed games and later extensions are interpretations, with their own eras.
4. New terrain, travel connections and missing details are **own additions**.

The web pages below were read. A complete frame-by-frame review of the released
films has **not** been performed in this delivery. Scene rows below identify
the required primary film and what must be checked against it; they do not claim
measured film dimensions. A real filming location is a landscape reference,
not the entire fictional island. No fan map or game mechanic establishes canon.

## Era and scale decisions

- `classic_adventure`: Nublar primarily 1993; Sorna combines 1997 and 2001;
  outer islands are explicitly designed extensions. This is a playable composite.
- `world_2015`: reserved additional profile using the same base geography.
  Historical replacement footprints must be mutually exclusive. The 1993
  visitor-center position gets the intact structure in `classic_adventure`
  and its later ruin in `world_2015`, never both. Reserving a profile does not
  mean its structures exist or can already be selected.
- A future eruption is a separate scenario with explicit migration and player
  data preservation. It does not reset the classic world automatically.
- The 32,768-block square, border, island sizes and shortened crossings are
  gameplay design. Sorna is the largest wilderness and Nublar the second focus.
  Do not label those block counts canonical island measurements.
- Film-scale terrain and client sight distance are evaluated separately. Camera
  views must still communicate the scene with the actual tested view distance.

## Visual evidence labels

Every delivered image is labeled as one of: `heightmap`, `source-model render`,
`film/production reference`, `server-data map`, or `actual client screenshot`.
Record capture coordinates, yaw/pitch, version, profile and view distance for
client images. An AI image, model render or heightmap never proves blocks were
built in a saved world. Film stills and third-party assets are references only;
this register grants no redistribution rights.
