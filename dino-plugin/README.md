# ARK dinos

Small server plugin for Paper **26.3 build 244**, Java **25**. Native Sniffers and Ravagers remain the gameplay entities and mounts. The optional BetterModel/PacketEvents/GeyserModelEngine presentation stack attaches the eight custom models when available; without it, native taming and riding remain usable. Geyser clients use their normal interact, jump and sneak controls; Bedrock/PS5 rendering and device input still need a real client test.

The pinned GeyserModelEngine1.0.9 task-publication fix has a [source patch, reproducible Java25 rebuild and native regression](upstream-patches/README.md). It changes only the bridge's task initialization; public dependency pins remain unchanged.

Outside `prototype-worlds`, interact with an `ark_dino` using one torchflower seed in the main hand. One seed is consumed and your UUID is saved on the existing entity. Sneak and interact again, with any main-hand item, to toggle stay/follow. Other players cannot change ownership, command or leash the pet. Creative players cannot acquire or command Survival pets. Wild Ravagers retain their normal attacks.

With an empty main hand, interact with your tamed Sniffer or Ravager to ride it. Use normal forward/backward/strafe controls and look to steer; sneak to dismount. Only its Survival owner can mount an unoccupied pet in an allowed world. In the prototype test world, sneak+interact on foot with an empty hand opens Follow, Stay and Free wander, plus a separately confirmed Release action. Free wander retains ownership and owner defense; Stay stops movement and defense. Only adult, unmounted animals can be released; the same entity enters the controlled wild roaming behavior and they remain peaceful toward their latest former owner across restarts. Outside the prototype world, sneak+interact keeps the legacy stay/follow toggle. Riding pauses follow pathfinding and preserves the saved stay setting. Ground movement uses conservative walk speed and faster native sprint input, keeps native gravity and collisions, and stops before unloaded chunks. Ground species cannot fly. There is no saddle or extra control setting. Logout, death, mode/world changes, pet unloading and plugin shutdown end control and remove passengers.

The Pteranodon alone can fly: jump takes off and ascends, look and use directional controls to steer, and look down while moving forward to descend and land. Releasing controls hovers. Ascent stops at 48 blocks above takeoff or below the world ceiling, whichever is lower. Native collisions remain active; rider headroom is reserved, water ends flight, and an unloaded destination stops all velocity without loading chunks. Sneak dismounts; the mount descends under control. Flight exits give the former rider and mount bounded slow falling long enough to reach the world's minimum height, including when unload or shutdown interrupts landing. Gravity is restored on landing, unload, death, disable and stale flight-state recovery; fall-distance resets apply only during controlled flight/landing. Normal damage and void damage still apply.

Adult wild Pteranodon and owned animals in Free mode make short low flights between prechecked, loaded, dry landing sites. Their full wing sweep is checked. A positive, uncancelled player or player-arrow hit requests landing and at least sixty seconds on the ground; accepted taming feeds keep it grounded for the saved taming period. Babies and Follow mode remain on the ground. Stay lands safely, and a rider always takes over manual control. No extra creatures or habitat groups spawn automatically.

Native name tags retain their normal item use and owner checks. Each nonempty custom name is shown by one native nonpersistent TextDisplay above a head anchor, following growth, animation and flight. All viewers see updates and reconnects; clearing the name or unloading the model removes the label. No BetterModel private name-label cache is used.

Prototype ground paces, in blocks per second:

| Species | Walk | Sprint / fast Follow and chase |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Brachiosaurus | 1.0 | 2.0 |
| Stegosaurus | 1.0 | 2.8 |
| Triceratops | 1.4 | 4.6 |
| T-Rex | 1.5 | 6.0 |
| Velociraptor | 2.0 | 7.0 |
| Dilophosaurus | 1.8 | 6.0 |
| Pteranodon on ground | 1.4 | 3.0 |

Wild/Free wandering uses Walk; Follow and combat use the faster pace. Mounted normal/sprint input uses the same values. Releasing Sprint returns to Walk, and released movement input stops the mount. Pteranodon flight retains a seven-block-per-second cruise with either input. Outside prototype worlds legacy native movement is unchanged. These are conservative game approximations, not known fossil maximum speeds. The T-Rex walk is informed by [the 2021 preferred-walking-speed model](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.201441); trackways provide [slow-walking examples](https://www.app.pan.pl/article/item/app46-235.html), with substantial uncertainty when inferring speed.


To enable custom dinosaurs only in selected worlds while keeping other pets native, include the allowed worlds in `survival-worlds` and list only model worlds in `prototype-worlds`:

```yaml
survival-worlds: [ark, custom_dinos]
prototype-worlds: [custom_dinos]
```

`prototype-worlds` limits new species identification/migration, models, Pteranodon flight, advanced taming, breeding, eggs and dinosaur combat. Outside it, existing ownership, tame/stay/follow and native ground riding continue, and saved species cannot enable flight. Omit the setting to use `survival-worlds`; set `[]` to disable prototype features everywhere. A persisted active-flight marker still restores gravity on load for safety. Removing an allowlist entry does not delete its world or entities.

Eight species persist as `arkdinos:species`: `triceratops`, `stegosaurus`, `trex`, `brachiosaurus`, `pteranodon`, `velociraptor`, `dilophosaurus`, `mosasaurus`. Exact legacy habitat tags `ark_renewal_habitat_01_dino_0` through habitat 12, slot 1, map those species in that repeating order: four entities per species across the 24 tags. T-Rex, Velociraptor and Dilophosaurus use Ravagers; the four rideable herbivore/flying species use Sniffers. Mosasaurus uses a Dolphin and remains wild. The 24 legacy habitat tags retain their original six-species mapping; Dilophosaurus is identified by its persisted species. Compatible saved species win over habitat tags. Conflicting or incompatible tags use a logged native-type default (Triceratops or T-Rex), as do untagged legacy owned dinos. Ordinary untagged mobs remain unchanged. Migration updates PDC/model attachment on the existing entity, preserving UUID, health, position, ownership and stay state, without respawning or teleporting it. Tagged wild dinos also receive models on enable, entity load and supported spawn events.

Outside prototype worlds, owned dinos are passive, including Ravagers' damage and target selection. Prototype carnivores have the controlled combat behavior below. They follow their online Survival owner in the same world, between 3 and 48 blocks away, using native pathfinding. They stop when staying, the owner leaves, changes world or enters Creative, or goes beyond 48 blocks. Come back near a pet to resume following. Pets do not teleport, load chunks or cross portals/worlds. Native pathfinding cannot guarantee passage through fences, cliffs or deep water; stay pauses AI but does not pin an entity against pushing or water currents.

Attached models switch between idle and walk from measured horizontal movement on the ground, including wild, following and ridden dinos. Java and Bedrock use a continuous walking clock with a bounded cadence: the cadence follows actual movement and stays between 0.35 and 1.8 cycles per second at all species paces. Growth does not accelerate the gait. Ground Pteranodon wings fold; flight unfolds them. The Bedrock model and its mounted native carrier share the same movement packet stream to keep the rider fixed during turns. Holding movement into a wall does not keep a stationary model walking, and falling does not trigger walk. Airborne Pteranodon flight keeps its explicit flap/glide phase.

Only an explicitly placed dinosaur egg creates a new dinosaur. The plugin never respawns a dead or unloaded pet. Ownership and stay state persist through normal world saves/restarts as entity PDC: `arkdinos:owner` (UUID string), `arkdinos:stay` (byte), `arkdinos:species` (string). `arkdinos:flight` is a temporary persisted recovery marker, removed when gravity is restored. Only currently loaded pets are kept in memory; their paths update once per second. Load restores their controller. Habitat datapacks must not teleport `ark_dino_tamed` back to their home, or respawn replacement groups when pets leave.

Sniffers use a Brain rather than ordinary goal selectors. A small Minecraft 26.3 adapter clears their native Brain behaviors once on tame/load, allowing Paper's Pathfinder to control them. This also replaces natural sniffing/digging behavior on owned Sniffers. The adapter must be checked after Minecraft upgrades; failure stops a pet safely and refuses new Sniffer taming without consuming food. Ordinary unmanaged Sniffers retain native AI. Tagged prototype wild and owned animals use controlled pathfinding without native digging. Food supports taming, breeding and growth; healing is omitted.

## Prototype gameplay

These features apply only inside `prototype-worlds`; existing owners and animals are retained. T-Rex, Velociraptor and Dilophosaurus need five feeds of raw beef, pork, chicken, mutton or rabbit, ten seconds apart, then a bone. Pteranodon needs five feeds of raw cod, salmon or tropical fish, ten seconds apart, then seagrass. The three herbivores need eight feeds of wheat, fern or dandelion in any combination, fifteen seconds apart, then a torchflower seed. Progress is visible and saved for five minutes after the latest accepted feed; another player cannot take over during that time. Wrong food and early clicks consume nothing.

Feed two owned adults of the same species, with the same owner, within six blocks. Each parent needs one primary food and has a five-minute breeding cooldown. A successful pair puts a named species egg into the owner's ordinary inventory; overflow drops at the owner. `/dinoeier` opens the eight-species egg selection in Creative mode inside a prototype world. Seven entries are fertile eggs; Mosasaurus is a separate creative spawn egg for a wild animal in deep, unobstructed water. Each species has its own inventory/held-item graphic on Java and Bedrock; placed nests retain the native Sniffer egg block and use a distinct species shell model on Java and Bedrock. Loaded nests have one nonpersistent, noninteractive marker; breaking, hatching, unloading or stopping the plugin removes only that presentation. Missing optional model plugins leave the native egg usable. Select an egg to receive a real inventory item; it is usable on Java and Bedrock without a custom Creative tab. Eggs preserve their creator's ownership when transferred.

Place an egg where normal block placement is permitted. Normal incubation takes twenty minutes and growth fifteen minutes; in prototype test worlds both take five minutes. Existing nests and juveniles retain elapsed age, feeding acceleration, species, owner and UUID when the saved duration changes. Unloaded chunks are never forced to load. A blocked or cancelled hatch keeps its egg for a later attempt. Hatchlings start at 45% size. Triceratops horns, Stegosaurus plates/tail spikes, Pteranodon crest and Dilophosaurus crests/frill use three detail stages: hatchling, juvenile after half the growth period, and adult. Feeding advances the same saved age and detail stage. Young owned animals can follow and wander; they cannot be ridden, released, bred or used in combat until adult. Feeding a hatchling one accepted primary food removes one minute from its remaining growth time, with a thirty-second feeding cooldown. Survival breaking returns one species egg and restarts incubation when placed again. Explosions destroy the egg; pistons cannot move a nest. Ordinary Sniffer eggs remain unchanged. The model test world's moss area is the permitted hatching surface; the arena floor and other regions retain their protections.

Wild T-Rex, Velociraptors and Dilophosaurs attack nearby Survival players. Other wild species remain passive. Owned adults defend their owner and attack the owner's opponent, including targets hit by an arrow. While riding, the attack button plays the species attack at most once per second, including misses. Damage requires an aimed, visible target within melee reach. A mounted owner can explicitly attack while Stay remains selected; after dismounting, Stay still prevents automatic combat. Dilophosaurus bites and scratches in melee and spits black slime at visible, more distant targets. An allowed hit briefly blinds and slows the target. The seven rideable species have a matching finite combat animation: bite, claw rake, horn thrust, tail swipe, stomp or beak jab. Stay cancels autonomous combat immediately. Dinosaurs cannot attack their owner, owned pets or Creative/Spectator players. Player targets additionally require world PvP, Survival mode, team friendly fire permission and WorldGuard PvP permission at the owner, dinosaur and target. When GriefPrevention is installed, its owner-attributed permission checks also protect fresh spawns, safe claims and trusted animals; only a successful real hit commits its combat/logout state. Native damage events still occur once and remain cancellable by protection plugins. A broken installed WorldGuard or GriefPrevention integration denies companion attacks and logs one warning.

## Wild Mosasaurus

Mosasaurus is a separate, water-only prototype species. Its original model is twenty-four blocks long, with four flippers, a muscular tail, a vertical tail fin and a swimming/bite cycle. This deliberately gigantic game scale is not a scientific size claim. The native Dolphin carrier has scale4 and health200. It roams loaded water, occasionally makes a vertical breach where its full body has a clear loaded water/air corridor, and returns to a checked submerged landing. A cooldown limits jumps; obstruction and unload/restart recovery preserve the same entity. It may attack Survival swimmers with cancellable bites; Creative/Spectator players and players on land are excluded. Its visible body, flippers and tail must stay in loaded water. The prototype creates no automatic ocean population or replacement animals.

Mosasaurus cannot be tamed, ridden, leashed or bred, and is excluded from the seven fertile care species. Its separate Creative-menu egg spawns a wild Mosasaurus only in loaded deep water, with normal protection checks. In the test basin, the permitted underwater spawn area is X128–144, Y48–56, Z−8–8; breaking blocks remains denied there. Feeding interactions consume nothing and do not start native Dolphin treasure behavior. Existing ordinary Dolphins remain unchanged. The model-test setup adds one explicit wild specimen to a separate eastern glass-sided basin, with a protected walkway; it does not replace the existing seven land/flying models or their entities. The main-world oceans are unchanged during this test.

## Build

Run `rtk proxy bash dino-plugin/build.sh` from the repository root with JDK 25 in `JAVA_HOME`, or use the local verified JDK at `dino-plugin/.tools/jdk-25.0.5+8/Contents/Home`. Build dependencies are downloaded with fixed SHA-256 checksums and kept outside Git. Cloudflare DoH is used for these downloads because the current VPN DNS was unreachable; it changes no system DNS setting.

Output: `dino-plugin/build/ArkDinos-1.1.0.jar`. Copy that single JAR to the server's `plugins/` directory and restart normally. The generated `plugins/ArkDinos/config.yml` allows only `ark`; add other Survival worlds explicitly if wanted. Do not hot-reload plugins.

The build runs dependency-free checks for ownership, Survival restrictions, food consumption, stay/follow, mounting restrictions, directional movement, all 24 legacy species tags, deterministic migration, flight math and passenger packet ordering. These checks do not replace the native runtime checks below.

`rtk proxy node dino-plugin/model-runtime-test.mjs` starts a fresh loopback Paper fixture with the optional model stack and current eight model exports. It rebuilds the generated Bedrock pack, checks actual native ground displacement against BetterModel walk/idle phases, wall-blocked motion, falling, airborne flap/glide, phase deduplication and model reload. Its native seat measurements are Java checks; they do not prove Bedrock/PS5 seating or appearance. Set `DINO_MODEL_OUTPUT` to an absolute output directory to keep separate reports; the default is `output/dino-adapter-runtime-20261107/runtime-report.json`.

`rtk proxy node dino-plugin/riding-runtime-test.mjs` creates a fresh loopback-only Paper 26.3/236 fixture under `dino-plugin/build/riding-smoke-*`, compiles a test-only fixture plugin and stops its server afterward. It reads cached Paper bootstrap/libraries from `artifacts/renewal-20261103/` without opening that historical server's worlds. Set `DINO_PAPER_JAR` and `DINO_PAPER_CACHE` for another matching local cache. It uses direct Java protocol777 test clients and no Node dependencies. The report is `dino-plugin/build/riding-runtime-report.json`. Chunk unloading is a fixture-dispatched `EntitiesUnloadEvent`; official Java, Bedrock/PS5 clients, actual ridden chunk unloading and difficult terrain still need playtesting.

Verified on 2026-11-07: all eight riding smoke checks passed, including both native species mounting through actual interaction packets, forward input moving each about 4.4 blocks per second on flat ground, unchanged height, sneak dismount, ownership/item/mode/passenger denial, preserved stay, mode/world/logout/death cleanup, the unload-event fixture and native plugin disable. Follow paths remained stopped while mounted; no chunks were force-loaded and no ArkDinos errors occurred. Native collision pushes after dismount remain normal physics.

`rtk proxy node dino-plugin/gameplay-runtime-test.mjs` creates a separate fresh Paper world under `dino-plugin/build/gameplay-smoke-*`. It checks all 24 migrations and native identity/state invariants, compatible PDC and conflict handling, actual input-packet takeoff/hover/pitch descent/landing/remount, owner/mode restrictions, flight exits and gravity recovery, the altitude cap, rider head obstruction, water, unloaded-destination XYZ stopping, and a clean server restart. `build/gameplay-runtime-report.json` includes actual server position/NoGravity samples in `flightSamples` for takeoff, hover, steering/descent and landing. Chunk lifecycle and the unloaded-destination guard use test-only native fixtures; this does not substitute for actual distant chunk unloading or Bedrock/PS5 device QA. Both isolated harnesses stop their own server processes afterward. To repeat gameplay with the exact optional stack and all eight assets, run `rtk proxy env DINO_MODEL_STACK=1 node dino-plugin/gameplay-runtime-test.mjs`; it uses the cached Paper 26.3/241 + BetterModel 3.6.0 + PacketEvents 2.14.0 + GeyserModelEngine 1.0.9 + Geyser 2.12.1-b1295 stack in a fresh world, checks model trackers and actual flight animation selection, and writes `build/gameplay-model-runtime-report.json`. It reads the local spike cache but never opens its world.

The repository's loopback preview has an automated real-client harness: `rtk proxy node dino-plugin/runtime-test.mjs`, followed by a parent-controlled server restart and `rtk proxy node dino-plugin/runtime-test.mjs --verify-persistence`. Its guard checks `artifacts/renewal-20261103/server/server.properties`; it uses local RCON 25585, ViaProxy 25578 and separate `RenewalDino`/`RenewalDinoB` profiles. It creates temporary fixtures at X/Z 100–145, Y 200–210. `--resume` reuses passed baseline checks and existing fixtures after a harness interruption. `--cleanup` removes those fixtures and keeps the result reports. The current Mineflayer version requires `useOn()` for sneak interactions because `activateEntity()` hardcodes a non-sneaking click.

Verified on 2026-11-03: 11 baseline checks and 6 checks after a real server restart passed, including real clicks, one-seed charging, both species following without food held, sneak toggling, ownership denial, preserved entity UUIDs/state without duplicates, restored Sniffer following, post-restart Ravager passivity and cancellation of native cross-world teleports. A separate wild Ravager damaged the test player, confirming that the passivity test could detect combat damage. That baseline JAR SHA-256 was `ffe1e69b29189dffc6c741552da225823414f5349ee739f8864469857ab1aa6a`. Test mobs, drops and the sky platform were removed afterward; the normal habitat dinos remained. Reports are `runtime-report.json`, `runtime-report-persistence.json` and `runtime-report-cleanup.json` beside this file (local build evidence, ignored by Git). PS5/Bedrock controls and difficult terrain still require real-device playtesting.

## Native runtime checks

The following one-seed/passive baseline applies outside `prototype-worlds`. Advanced care and combat have separate isolated native harnesses: `node dino-plugin/care-runtime-test.mjs` and `node dino-plugin/combat-runtime-test.mjs`.

On an isolated Paper 26.3/236 test server, use two ordinary non-OP players and a safe flat test area in `ark`. Keep pets away from habitat respawn functions during the test.

1. Confirm `version ArkDinos` and no plugin errors in the log. Summon one tagged Sniffer and one tagged Ravager with `Tags:["ark_dino","ark_dino_wild"]` and `PersistenceRequired:1b`. Also keep one untagged control mob.
2. Give player A two torchflower seeds. Interact with each tagged mob in Survival: ownership must equal A's UUID, each interaction removes exactly one seed, and `ark_dino_tamed` replaces `ark_dino_wild`. Untagged mobs must remain unowned. Try in Creative first: no owner and no seed consumption.
3. Player B tries to tame, sneak-command and leash A's pets: their owner and stay state must not change and B's seeds must remain. In the prototype world A sneaks with an empty hand and selects Follow, Stay or Free wander; items must not be consumed. Release needs a second confirmation and preserves the animal's identity. Outside the prototype world, sneak+interact keeps the legacy stay/follow toggle.
4. A walks 10 blocks on flat ground without holding seeds. Each following pet must approach; in stay mode it must remain nearby when A moves. Test owner logout, Creative, changing to `ark_creative`, and returning near the pet. No chunk should be force-loaded and no cross-world pet should appear.
5. In Survival without resistance, A and B approach the owned Ravager and provoke it. Health must remain unchanged by that Ravager, including roar, and a nearby owned wolf must not be targeted. A separate wild Ravager must still attack; test it in a separate enclosed area.
6. Save and restart the server. Query the same entity UUIDs: owner and stay bytes must remain, total entity count must not increase, and the owned Ravager must remain passive after provocation. Unload and reload a pet's chunk and repeat follow/stay.
7. Empty A's main hand, interact with each pet and steer in all four directions. Confirm no follow path runs while riding, diagonal travel is not faster, no flight occurs for ground species even with jump, and sneak dismounts without changing stay. B, Creative A, held items and wild/occupied dinos must refuse mounting. Repeat logout, death, world/mode change, chunk unload and plugin shutdown while mounted; riders and horizontal control must clear. Attempt to add another entity as a passenger: it must be rejected.
